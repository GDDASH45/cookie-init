# HSLDD: Helping Small Linux Distro Developers

HSLDD (Helping Small Linux Distro Developers) is an open-source initiative dedicated to helping small and independent Linux distributions grow into successful, functional, and useful operating systems.

Our goal is to provide tools, resources, and software that make Linux distribution development more accessible. Whether you're building your first Linux distro or maintaining an existing project, HSLDD aims to help you overcome common development challenges and bring your vision to life.

We believe that every Linux distribution deserves the opportunity to grow, regardless of the size of its development team or community. By sharing knowledge, creating reusable tools, and collaborating with other developers, we can make it easier to build, improve, and maintain independent Linux distributions.

## How You Can Help

If you wish, we welcome you to contribute to this project! Contributions of all kinds are appreciated, including source code, documentation, bug reports, testing, ideas, and suggestions for improvement.

You do not need to maintain a large project or be an experienced developer to participate. Every useful contribution can help make Linux distribution development easier for someone else.

## Our Mission

- Help small Linux distributions become more functional and reliable.
- Provide reusable tools and resources for distro developers.
- Encourage open-source collaboration and knowledge sharing.
- Make operating-system development more approachable for independent developers.
- Support the growth of diverse Linux distributions and their communities.

## Get Involved

Explore the repository, try the available tools, report issues, suggest improvements, or contribute code. If you are developing your own Linux distribution, you are also welcome to use this project as a resource for your work.

**Together, we can help small Linux distributions grow into something useful.**

Thank you for your interest in HSLDD and for supporting independent Linux development!
